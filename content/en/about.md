This knowledge base serves three purposes:

1. Compress research projects into the reusable structure of problem, insight, algorithm, result, and boundary.
2. Decompose long papers into a knowledge network that can be studied chapter by chapter and checked through individual numerical examples.
3. Recover the data flow, control flow, parameter flow, and reliability loop of large engineering systems from their codebases.

## Levels of Evidence

| Label                  | Meaning                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Proven                 | Supported by a theorem, derivation, or strict equivalence                       |
| Validated              | Supported by code, a fixed protocol, and inspectable numerical results          |
| Engineering constraint | Imposed by the current implementation, configuration, or deployment boundary    |
| To be confirmed        | Still requires multiple seeds, ablations, online rollout, or owner confirmation |

The notes explicitly distinguish mathematical guarantees, experimental observations, and engineering experience instead of presenting all three as the same kind of conclusion.

## Information Security

The site source must not contain passwords, keys, cookies, internal addresses, personally identifiable information, or real business samples. The OneRec section should be moved to a private knowledge base or removed from the public site after internal review. Because old material can remain in Git history, deleting a page does not retract a disclosure; the full commit history must be audited again before publication.
